Yeh Feature Kya Karta Hai?
Jab koi reader novel card ke neeche "Read Online" button par click karega,
to ek clean aur attractive pop-up modal open hoga jisme 2 options honge:

1. 🇵🇰 Urdu Edition (اردو ایڈیشن):
   Is par click karne se tamam episodes (aqsaat) ki list open hogi jahan se reader
   Episode 1, Episode 2 waghaira direct parh sakta hai.

2. 🇬🇧 English Edition (انگلش ایڈیشن):
   Filhal yeh "Coming Soon..." par set hai. Jab is par click karein to notification
   samne aati hai ke English translation jald release hogi.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1️⃣ Kisi Bhi Novel Par Yeh Pop-up ON (Enable) Ya OFF (Disable) Karne Ka Tareeqa:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
File open karein: js/data/novels-list.js
Us novel ke block ke andar yeh setting dekhein:

* Pop-up ON karne ke liye:
    hasLanguageSelection: true,

* Pop-up OFF karne ke liye:
    hasLanguageSelection: false,

(Note: Jab false hoga to language pop-up show nahi hoga aur reader ke click karne
par direct novel ka main link open hoga).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2️⃣ "The Last Letter" Ya Kisi Novel Mein Nayi Episode (Episode 3, 4 waghaira) Add Karna:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
File open karein: js/data/novels-list.js
Novel ke andar "languages" -> "urdu" -> "episodes" ki list mein jayein.
Wahan Episode 2 ke baad comma (,) lagakar yeh naya block paste karein:

{
    episodeNumber: 3,
    title: "Episode 3: New Title",
    nativeTitle: "قسط نمبر 3",
    releaseDate: "16 Sep 2026",
    link: "https://thelastletterds.blogspot.com/apka-episode-3-link.html",
    isAvailable: true
},

4️⃣ English Edition ON Karne Ka Tareeqa (Jab Available Ho Jaye):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
English Edition block mein ye tabdeeli karein:
    isAvailable: true,
    statusBadge: "Available Now",
    link: "https://your-english-translation-link.com",

Is se reader jab English Edition par tap karega to direct English book open ho jayegi!
