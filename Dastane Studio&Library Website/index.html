Our Story

Dastane Studio & Library was born from a simple belief — that regional voices deserve a global stage. What began as a small circle of passionate writers has grown into a dedicated publishing studio and digital library preserving the richness of Urdu literature.

We nurture emerging talent, publish carefully crafted novels and short stories, and make them accessible to readers everywhere. Every title we release carries the soul of our soil, our folklore, and our people.

Publish Your Novel or Story With Us

Are you an aspiring writer with an unreleased novel, episodic series, or emotional short story? We welcome you to collaborate and publish on Dastane Studio with full author credit, cover design support, and reader engagement.

  • Manuscript Publishing: Get your original Urdu novels, thrillers, or short stories featured in our official digital library.
  • Editorial & Cover Design Support: Our team assists with literary proofing, synopsis refinement, and aesthetic book cover artwork.
  • 100% Original Author Credit: Every story and novel submitted will be officially published strictly under your own author name with full copyright respect and an individual author spotlight.
  • Growing Reader Community: Reach thousands of passionate fiction enthusiasts across Pakistan and worldwide.
